# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss ideas constructively and make participation
accessible. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information
are unacceptable in project spaces and when representing the project.

Maintainer @4ezzy handles reports and may remove content, restrict participation,
or exclude contributors according to the severity and circumstances. Request a
private contact channel without posting incident details publicly. Reports are
handled confidentially to the extent needed to investigate and respond.
